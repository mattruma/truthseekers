# Truth Seekers Club

**Seek truth. Think carefully. Listen well.**

The **Truth Seekers Club** is a group dedicated to exploring important questions about truth, morality, faith, philosophy, science, history, politics, and culture through respectful and logical discussion.

Our goal is not to win arguments, but to **seek truth, challenge assumptions, examine evidence, and learn from one another.**

---

## About the Club

Important questions deserve thoughtful conversation.

We bring together people with different backgrounds, perspectives, and beliefs to investigate ideas with intellectual honesty.

Participants are encouraged to:

- Ask difficult questions.
- Use reason and evidence.
- Explain the reasoning behind their beliefs.
- Represent opposing viewpoints fairly.
- Distinguish facts from interpretations and opinions.
- Listen carefully to others.
- Admit uncertainty when the evidence is unclear.
- Be willing to revise a belief when the evidence warrants it.

---

## Guiding Principles

### Seek Truth, Not Victory

The purpose of discussion is to discover what is true or most reasonably supported, not simply to win an argument.

### Use Reason and Evidence

Claims should be supported by clear reasoning, relevant evidence, and sound logic.

### Represent Opposing Views Fairly

We should engage the strongest reasonable version of an opposing position rather than a caricature of it.

### Separate People From Ideas

Ideas and arguments may be challenged vigorously while every participant is treated with dignity and respect. Personal attacks are not acceptable.

### Make Room for Uncertainty

Not every question has a simple or certain answer. Our confidence should reflect the strength of the available evidence.

### Be Willing to Change

Intellectual honesty requires a willingness to reconsider even deeply held beliefs when there are good reasons to do so.
