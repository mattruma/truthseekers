# Tu Quoque ("You Too")

## Description

Responds to an argument by accusing the person making it of hypocrisy instead of addressing the argument.

## Example

> **Parent:** Smoking is dangerous to your health.
>
> **Teenager:** You smoked when you were my age, so you can't tell me smoking is dangerous.

## Explanation

The parent's past behavior may reveal inconsistency, but it does not establish that the claim about smoking is false.

A hypocrite can still make a true statement.
