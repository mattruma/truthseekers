# Biased Sample

## Description

Draws a conclusion about a population using a sample that does not adequately represent that population.

## Example

> An online poll of our website's readers found that 90% of people support our position. Therefore, 90% of Americans support it.

## Explanation

The website's readers may differ significantly from the general population.

A sample must adequately represent the population before its results can reasonably be generalized to that population.
