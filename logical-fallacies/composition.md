# Composition

## Description

Assumes that what is true of individual parts must also be true of the whole.

## Example

> Every player on this basketball team is an excellent individual player, so this must be an excellent team.

## Explanation

Excellent individual players do not necessarily work well together as a team.

Characteristics of individual parts do not always transfer to the whole.
