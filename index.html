Important questions deserve thoughtful conversation.

The Truth Seekers Club explores questions about truth, morality, faith, philosophy, science, history, politics, and culture through respectful, logical discussion. The goal is not to win arguments, but to examine ideas carefully, challenge assumptions, and learn from one another.

About

A place for serious questions without unnecessary combat.

We bring together people with different backgrounds and beliefs to investigate ideas with intellectual honesty. Participants are encouraged to ask hard questions, explain their reasoning, represent opposing views fairly, and remain willing to revise their conclusions when the evidence calls for it.

Guiding principles

How we discuss difficult ideas.

1

Seek truth, not victory

Arguments are tools for testing ideas, not weapons for defeating people.

2

Use reason and evidence

Claims should be supported by clear reasoning, relevant evidence, and sound logic.

3

Represent views fairly

Engage the strongest reasonable form of opposing positions rather than caricatures.

4

Separate people from ideas

Critique arguments directly while treating participants with dignity and respect.

5

Make room for uncertainty

Not every question has a simple answer. Confidence should match the strength of the evidence.

6

Be willing to change

Intellectual honesty includes the willingness to revise deeply held beliefs when necessary.
